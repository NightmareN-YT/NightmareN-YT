## About Me

I'm Ningtam Niyon, an independent creator working across game development, digital art, 3D modeling, and software development.

I enjoy building interactive experiences, designing game systems, creating visual assets, and exploring different ways to bring ideas to life. My work combines technical development with visual creativity, from gameplay and world-building to illustration and digital design.

I'm currently developing **Isekai Story of Chiyo** and working on projects under **Ricefield Production**.
